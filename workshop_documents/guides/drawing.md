# Drawing: sheets, manuals, patent figures

## A drawing sheet
- `drawing_make` gives SVG, PDF and DXF files and a TechDraw page with the same views, on TechDraw's ISO 5457 template with its title block filled. Ask which projection the person works in: first angle (ISO, Europe) or third angle (ASME, North America). Left out, it follows TechDraw's preference, else inch units mean third angle; the answer says which (`projectionFrom`).
- Paper and scale are chosen together: the largest standard scale (1:1, 2:1, 1:2 …) at which the views, with room for their dimensions, fill no more than about 70% of the sheet, and the smallest paper at that scale. The iso view may get a smaller scale of its own (said under its name). `fill` in the answer is how full the sheet is.
- Only the overall sizes are dimensioned. Holes, slots and fits want dimensions placed in TechDraw by the person, on the page made.
- With FreeCAD's window up, the page opens as a tab before it is exported, because TechDraw only draws the template once a page has been shown. If the export still lacks it, the PDF and SVG come from Workshop's sheet writer (`filesFrom`). Overall sizes TechDraw measures differently from the part are left off and listed under `page.notOnPage`. The dimensions and hole callouts go on the TechDraw page itself, each on its own: one TechDraw cannot take is listed there too, by its view and text (`item`, `why`), and the rest still go on the page. Overall sizes run between ends of drawn edges at the view's extremes, never a circle's or arc's centre (TechDraw keeps those among the view's vertices, and a flat arc's centre lies outside the part); if the extremes measure otherwise, the next pair that measures the size is tried, each checked within 0.05 mm. Holes are matched to the view's circles by radius at the view's scale and by the hole's centre projected about the part's bounding-box centre, where TechDraw centres the view; a callout placed only by size carries a `warning` to check it in the GUI. Every view's lines are worked out before a dimension goes on it and again just before the export (TechDraw draws them on another thread and keeps the old ones readable meanwhile, so a page exported early held only the frame and the dimensions): `page.viewsReadyIn` and `page.exportViewsReadyIn` give the seconds, `page.viewsNotReady` names a view still not drawn after 30 s, and such a page is not exported. The SVG export is checked for the views' lines (`page.viewsInExport`); without them it is exported once more after a further wait, and if they are still missing the SVG and PDF come from Workshop's sheet writer, `filesFrom` saying why. TechDraw exports the PDF and SVG from the scene of the page's tab, and a view's lines are put on that scene only once the tab has been shown and painted; so before exporting, the page is shown, its tab made the active one and painted, each view asked to repaint (no recompute, which would start its lines afresh) and the event loop run (`page.exportScene` lists the steps done; `page.exportSceneAgain` before the second try). On the sheet writer's files a hole callout's leader lands only on a drawn circle of the hole's size (within 2%, a thread's tap drill accepted) where the hole projects; a hole with no such circle in its view (hidden there) is left off the sheet rather than pointed at a slot's end or a bore of another size, and listed under `calloutsOff` and as `calloutOff` in its `holes` row. Each hole callout's text and leaders clear every earlier callout's: texts tested against each other at the page's size (3.5 mm) with a margin, leaders never crossing, a later callout raised, turned to the other side or set at 30 or 60 degrees until it is clear.
- `file` can be a full path: its folder is made, and the PDF, SVG and DXF share its name without the extension.
- `holes: true` calls out every round hole once per size, with a leader, in a view that sees it end on (front, then right, then top …). FreeCAD's own Holes are called out as they are made: size or thread, THRU or ↧ depth, ⌴ counterbore or ⌵ countersink (`4× ⌀8 THRU ⌴⌀13 ↧8.5`). Holes no drawn view sees end on go in a hole table on the sheet.
- The title block's sample texts (names, numbers) are emptied, never filled with a made-up name. Give `drawnBy` only when the person tells you whose name goes there.
- The title block gives the material by its name, never by its id.
- `scale` is refused when it does not fit; leave it out for the largest standard scale that does.

## An assembly manual
- `drawing_exploded { steps: [{ title, add: [bodies], note }] }`, in build order. Bodies the same shape are counted as one item in the bill of materials.
- Record materials (`part_spec`) and setups first, or the bill has no mass or process for them.

## Patent figures and a draft
- `patent_figures` then `patent_draft`, so the draft uses the same numerals and figures.
- The draft is a starting point for a registered patent attorney or agent. Never present it as ready to file, and never file, send or upload it: that is the person's and counsel's.
- Formal drawing rules change, and an examiner may ask for changes. The figures follow the USPTO's margins and lettering size; say that is all they promise.

## Sections
- `drawing_make { section: "front" }` draws that view cut through the middle (`sectionAt` mm towards the viewer moves the plane): the material in front taken away, the cut faces hatched, titled SECTION A-A, and the cutting line drawn and lettered in a view that sees the plane edge on (top for a front section). The TechDraw page gets TechDraw's own section view.
- Cut through what matters: a bore's axis, a pocket's middle, the thinnest wall. Feature `dims` on that view measure the cut.
